About Devquiry

Software for people who just want things to work.

Devquiry creates practical digital tools designed to remove friction from everyday work.

Our story

Built out of irritation.

Devquiry exists because too much modern software is rented, bloated and needy. Tools demand accounts, subscriptions and tutorials before they do anything useful — then keep demanding attention long after.

We make the opposite: focused products you buy once, download immediately and own outright. Software with a job description — one it takes seriously. Each product starts from a real task someone struggles with weekly, and ships only when a new user can succeed with it in minutes.

It’s a deliberately small catalogue. We’d rather maintain six excellent tools than sixty average ones.

Philosophy

Four rules we don’t break.

01

Own what you buy

One-time pricing and perpetual licenses. Your software never stops working because a billing cycle ended.

02

Fast beats feature-rich

Speed and clarity are features. Anything that doesn’t help the task get done is left out on purpose.

03

Your data stays yours

Offline-first products, open export formats, and no behavioural analytics. Only license checks ever phone home.

04

Support by builders

When you email us, the person who wrote the code answers. It keeps support honest and the product better.
