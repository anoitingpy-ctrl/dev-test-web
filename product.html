Overview

RelayDesk is built for teams of 2–20 who handle real customer conversations but don’t want an enterprise suite. Everything lives in one calm, fast window: conversations, assignments, notes and history.

It runs offline-first on your own machine, syncs when you’re online, and never holds your data hostage. Export everything, any time, in open formats.

Features

  • Shared inboxEvery conversation, one queue, zero duplicates.
  • Collision detectionSee when a teammate is already replying.
  • Saved repliesReusable answers with variables, shared per team.
  • Assignment rulesRoute conversations automatically by sender or topic.
  • Offline-firstWork anywhere; syncs quietly when you reconnect.
  • Keyboard-drivenFull command palette and shortcuts for everything.
